About

Who We Are

Spear is a national youth employment charity dedicated to transforming the lives of young people aged 16–24 who face significant barriers to work. Through intensive coaching, mentoring, and employer connections, Spear equips participants not just with job-readiness skills, but with the confidence and resilience to sustain employment and grow in life.

Operating from 18 centres across the UK, Spear's evidence-based programme has supported over 1,200 young people a year — many of whom come from communities with high unemployment, poverty, or disadvantage — into sustainable work and further education.

🏆
Recent award — £160,000 grant (March 2026) Spear named one of five Global Recipients, enabling expansion of its coaching model to reach more unemployed young people across the UK.
The Programme

How the Spear Programme Works

Spear's core programme combines group coaching, one-to-one support, and employer engagement to move young people from unemployment to sustainable employment.

Step 1
Enrol & Assess
Young person joins; barriers and goals are mapped with a personal coach.
Step 2
Intensive Coaching
Group and 1:1 sessions build confidence, resilience, and job-readiness.
Step 3
Employer Connect
Mock interviews, employer introductions, and job application support.
Step 4
In-Work Support
Continued coaching for 12 months after placement to sustain employment.
