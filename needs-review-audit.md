## Appendix: Audit Methodology


### Checks Performed


1. **Bad URLs:** Identified grants with URLs pointing to generic homepages (no path, or just root domain)
2. **Relevance:** Flagged programmes that are clearly not for UK civil society
   - US-only programmes (e.g., Goldman Sachs 10,000 Small Businesses for US entrepreneurs)
   - Commercial/startup equity competitions (not grant funding)
3. **Duplicates:** Checked for exact title matches and variants from same funder
4. **Categorization:** Remaining grants segmented by source and funder

### No Exact Duplicates Found

No grants within the needs-review set have identical titles to approved grants. 
This suggests the scraper has already avoided major duplicates.
