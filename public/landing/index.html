Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

How do I find funding for a charity?

Tell Shoots who you are and who you help, and it searches live, verified funding opportunities across grants, social investment, programmes and in-kind support, showing only what your organisation is eligible for.

What grants can a CIC apply for?

CICs are eligible for many community funding programmes, lottery funds and foundation grants, but eligibility varies by fund. Shoots checks each opportunity against your structure so you only see grants a CIC can actually win.

How does grant matching work?

You set up a profile once, covering your legal structure, location, size, sector and funding stage. Every opportunity in the catalogue is checked against all five, with the reasons why you match shown alongside each result.

Is Shoots free?

Plans are being finalised now and will be priced for smaller organisations. Join the waitlist on this page to see launch pricing first.

Will I be able to try it before paying?

That's the plan, with the details confirmed at launch. Join the waitlist and you'll be the first to know. Either way you can sign up to our newsletter, with new funding opportunities in your sectors, and pick up where you left off whenever you're ready.

What kinds of funding does Shoots cover?

The full funding mix: grants, social investment, programmes such as incubators, accelerators, training and support, and in-kind support like pro bono services and donated tools.

How is Shoots different from other funding databases?

Most databases list everything and leave the checking to you. Shoots sources opportunities widely but only publishes what is live and verified, matched against your legal structure, location, size, sector and funding stage. You see fewer results, and far more of them are worth your time.

Does Shoots write my applications for me?

No, and that’s deliberate. Shoots helps you find the right opportunities, manage your pipeline and shape applications in your own words. AI helps with structure and reuse, but the voice stays yours. Funders reward authenticity.

Do you cover the whole of the UK?

Yes. The catalogue spans UK-wide funders alongside national and regional funds for England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.

Who can see my pipeline and data?

Only your organisation. Your pipeline, projects and applications are private to your account. Other users and funders can’t see them, and we never share them. We only access account data when needed to support you.

What is a CIC?

A community interest company: a limited company that exists to benefit the community rather than shareholders. If you run one or are setting one up, Shoots shows the funding open to your structure.
