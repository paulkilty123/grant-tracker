# Handoff: Scotland, the funding.scot seam

Written 2026-09-11 by the orchestrating session, commissioned by Paul the
same afternoon as the first batch of the catalogue scale plan
(`catalogue-scale-plan-2026-09-11.md`, lever 4). Read `CLAUDE.md` first, all
of it, then `programmes-cohorts-2026-09-08.md` for the budget, citation and
staging rules, then `regions-cf-2026-09-09.md` for the shape of a regions
batch. All of it applies here unchanged. Then this.

## Why this job exists

Scotland is the thinnest nation in the catalogue for its size. Foundation
Scotland holds 19 rows with 9 live and does its job as the community
foundation. Below it the place layer is nearly empty: no council community
fund, no city trust, one Highland environment funder hidden, and the large
Scottish trusts that a Glasgow or Dundee charity would name first are held
one row at a time or not at all. The Robertson Trust, Gannochy and Corra are
in. Most of the rest of the list below is not.

funding.scot is the seam: a Salesforce-backed directory of every Scottish
fund with stable URLs, read once by hand in May 2026 for fifteen rows. The
scraper was never built. This job walks it by hand, funder-first, with the
filter the May ingest learned the expensive way
(`project_funding_scot_scraper_design`).

## The job in one sentence

Find the Scottish trusts, foundations and council funds that a charity, CIC
or social enterprise in Scotland can apply to today or within 30 days, that
the catalogue does not hold, and stage each one hidden for review.

**Target: 25 staged.** Stop there, or when the provider list is worked
through, or under the budget rule.

## The shape that qualifies

The three tests from the regions brief, unchanged:

1. **A charity, CIC or social enterprise applies.** Individuals-only,
   schools-only or business-only funds are `out_of_scope`. Under Paul's
   ruling of 10 September a programme open to any business counts when a
   trading social enterprise could join
   (`feedback_programmes_include_social_enterprises_ltd_by_shares`).
2. **The page states who can apply and what for.** A trust whose only page
   is the OSCR register entry has no applyable route: record it as
   `no_apply_route` and move on. Do not stage a register page.
3. **Open now, rolling, or opening within 30 days** with a stated date.
   Closed with no reopening date goes to the results file as
   `closed_no_date`, not to staging.

And the May filter, which is the one that saves the review queue: skip
signposting services, paid training, volunteer-only programmes, sunset
funders, and any directory entry with no named programme behind it.

## Tagging

`location_tag` is `Scotland` for national Scottish funders; the council area
or city as the funder names it for place funds (`Glasgow`, `Highland`,
`Fife`). `is_local: true` on every row, nations included, as the 54 Scotland
rows already do. `funder_type` as the provider is: `trust`,
`community_foundation`, `local_authority`, `corporate_foundation`. Eligible
structures exactly as the page states them; Scottish charities are SCIOs and
registered charities, so `scio` belongs on the list whenever the page says
"registered charity" and the funder is Scottish. Amounts only for a stated
per-applicant figure; read `feedback_pounds_on_a_page_are_rarely_the_award`
first.

## Budget, citations, dedup, staging, results

As `programmes-cohorts-2026-09-08.md`: one session, no fan-out, two page
reads per candidate, one search per provider, no Anthropic spend, batches of
ten with a dry run and tsc before each commit. Quotes only from page text
fetched in this session. A supabase-js read returns at most 1,000 rows
whatever `.limit()` says: page the table with `.range()` and refuse to dedup
against a partial read (`scripts/provider-walk-tier3-stage-2026-09-10.ts`
has the pattern).

Stage with `stampNewGrant`, `is_active: false`, `pipeline_state:
'tagged_awaiting_review'`, source `system:scotland-2026-09-11` at trust 50.
Staged rows land under "Needs reading" until the 01:00 engine reads them;
that is correct, never write `_page_read`.

Results in `docs/handoffs/scotland-results-2026-09-11.json`, the usual shape,
with `why` values from the programmes brief plus `closed_no_date`,
`front_door_only` and `no_apply_route`. Close with staged count per tier, the
funders walked, and the live Scotland row count before and after.

## What the orchestrator checks afterwards

Every staged URL opened and tested against the front-door rule; a sample of
staged rows re-read against the three tests; every quote checked against the
page it names; the dedup query re-run over staged titles, hosts and funders;
and confirmation that no existing row's `is_active` or `pipeline_state`
changed.
