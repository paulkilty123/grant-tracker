# The wrong-fund rows: verdicts after reading the pages

Drafted 2026-08-28, after your annotated draft. Nothing here has been applied.

Every row below was decided against a page opened and read today, in a real
browser where the fetcher was walled out. Where I could not get an answer from
the funder's own site I say so rather than infer one. Two verdicts disagree with
your annotations and one disagrees with mine of an hour ago, and both are marked.

**Headline: 7 keep (4 of them needing a link or title fix), 11 withdraw, 2 split,
1 scope decision.** Of the 14 recommended withdrawals, 8 survive contact with the
page. Four of the fourteen were recommended on pages nobody had ever read, and
three of those four turn out to carry open funds.


## Three things worth knowing before the row list

**The unread pages were the problem, exactly as you said.** Arts Council England,
Chichester, Crowdfunder and Visa CatalyseHer were all recommended for withdrawal
on a CAPTCHA, a cookie wall or a security check. All four read cleanly today, and
all four are live routes to money. CatalyseHer closes in ten days.

**The one recommendation you would have acted on unread is wrong.** The GMET
repoint target, `gmet.org.uk/apply-funding/renew-community-fund`, does not exist.
That site turns any unknown path into a site search, so a link checker looking
only at status codes will call it alive. Its sibling `/apply-funding` does the
same. Of tonight's three repoint proposals, one resolves and is right (Grocers'),
one proposes the URL the row already holds (Nesta), and one is this.

**A line worth setting, because it decides six rows.** Retitle only where the
label is a wrong name for the same single fund. Where the funder runs several
separately paged funds, the row is a generic entry and should be split, not
renamed, because renaming quietly turns one fund into another. That is your
Lloyds argument, and it applies equally to Film London and sportscotland, where
you argued for retitling. The difference you were protecting is real but it is
not a title problem: withdrawing a funder should be paired with staging its named
funds, not left as a hole.

### Chichester District Council - Community Grants Programme
*Chichester District Council* - https://www.chichester.gov.uk/grantprogramme

### Crowdfunder - Match Funding

## Keep, with a fix (4)

### Visa CatalyseHer Programme
*Visa Foundation & INCO* - currently https://inco.co.uk/catalyseher

- **KEEP, REPOINT, AND SET A DEADLINE.** This is the most expensive mistake in the
  draft: it was recommended for withdrawal on a CAPTCHA, and it is open with a
  deadline ten days out.
- Correct URL: **https://catalyseher-uk.inco-group.co/**. The inco.co.uk path is
  the wall, not the fund.
- "Applications are now open for Cohort 4, starting in September 2026."
  Applications close **7 September 2026**, programme starts 23 September.
- Microgrants "up to £5,000", with "$300k USD available in direct cash grants"
  across the programme.
- Eligibility is narrower than the row implies: women founders or CEOs of
  UK-registered businesses or HMRC sole traders, under five years old, under £2m
  revenue. This is business support with microgrants attached, not a charity grant,
  and should be tagged that way.

### Grocers' Charity Memorial Grant
*The Grocers' Charity* - currently https://grocershall.co.uk/the-charity/memorial-grants/

- **REPOINT, RETITLE, AND MARK BETWEEN ROUNDS.** The repoint was half right. The
  old URL is genuinely dead and `/the-charity/apply-for-a-grant` is the real route,
  but there is no scheme called a Memorial Grant, so repointing alone leaves a row
  with an invented name on a working link.
- Nine categories, not a memorial one: hardship, children and young people, elderly,
  disability and inclusion, health, military, arts, heritage, environment.
- "one-off grants of up to £5,000", UK-registered charities only, turnover under
  £500,000 (£15m for medical charities).
- Closed: the deadline was 17 July 2026 at noon and applications reopen **31 March
  2027**. So this is a between-rounds row, not a live one.

### Severn Trent Community Fund - Children's Football Clubs
*Severn Trent Community Fund* - https://www.stwater.co.uk/about-us/severn-trent-community-fund/

- **RETITLE AND REPOINT, DO NOT WITHDRAW.** There is no football or children's
  sport stream anywhere on the fund's pages. The label is invented. The funder is
  real, open, and running two properly named streams with their own pages.
- New Project Funding: £2,000 to £20,000 and £20,000 to £50,000, at
  `/about-us/severn-trent-community-fund/new-project-funding/`. "Our Community Fund
  is always open for applications", with panel deadlines. The next one is
  **31 August 2026**, three days away.
- Core Funding is the second stream, at `/core-funding/`, and deserves its own row.
- Eligibility: legally constituted not-for-profits, project in the Severn Trent
  region, beneficiaries must be Severn Trent customers.

### Bernard Sunley Foundation - Capital Grants
*Bernard Sunley Foundation* - https://www.bernardsunley.org/

- **KEEP, and repoint to `/how-to-apply/`.** Your APPLY stands. "we award around
  £5 million to capital projects throughout England and Wales", and the site
  carries How To Apply, an Eligibility Check and a Grant Application Form, so
  there is a real route in. Homepage is not wrong, but the apply page is better.
- I could not get the amounts or the eligibility text off `/how-to-apply/` today.
  The page is script-rendered and returned nothing readable in either the fetcher
  or the browser. Secondary sources describe it as rolling with no deadlines. That
  is not funder evidence, so treat the amounts as unknown rather than filling them.

### UK Sport - National Lottery Awards
*UK Sport* - https://www.uksport.gov.uk/our-work/investing-in-sport

- **WITHDRAW. I disagree with your retitle here.** There is no fund to retitle to.

*Film London* - https://filmlondon.org.uk/funding

### sportscotland - Community Sport
*sportscotland* - https://sportscotland.org.uk/funding/

## One scope decision, not a verification question (1)

### London Social and Affordable Homes Programme 2026 to 2036
*Greater London Authority* - https://www.london.gov.uk/.../london-social-and-affordable-homes-programme-2026-36

- **DO NOT WITHDRAW.** The page reads fine outside the fetcher. The programme is
  real and very large, up to £11.7bn.
- But two things are true that the row does not carry. Bidding opened February 2026
  and closed April 2026, so it is between rounds, not live. And the applicants are
  "local authorities, registered providers (not-for-profit and for-profit),
  unregistered bodies, and developers", which is a different audience from the rest
  of the catalogue.
- So the question is whether housing development programmes belong here at all. That
  is your call rather than the verifier's, and it probably governs more rows than
  this one.
