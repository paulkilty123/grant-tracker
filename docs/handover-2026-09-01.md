at Charity Commission register entries and passed every check the catalogue had:

in. **Harford Charitable Trust was live, published, carried no blocking reason,

listings and the sitemap; the public detail route read neither it nor
`pipeline_state`. So "hidden" meant "unfindable", and the gap was invisible from
the admin side because every admin surface reads `is_active` and agreed. **994
rejected/archived rows and 92 withheld rows were reachable by anyone with the
URL.** `src/lib/public-visibility.ts` fixes it; `src/lib/gone-grants.ts` makes the
terminal states answer 410.

## 3. Shipped

1. **Bot walls are no longer judged as pages.** `verify-row.ts` used
   `text.length < 200`; every wall measured is longer (Cloudflare 268 via proxy,
   491 direct; Imperva 678). 21 of 87 "the page does not describe this fund" rows
   were interception notices. `src/lib/verification/page-readable.ts` returns a
   discriminated union of eight reasons; `read.text` does not exist on the failure
   branch, so the compiler stops a verdict being derived from an unread page.
2. **Per-host backoff** (`host-backoff.ts`), shape C's ladder keyed by host, any
   success deletes the entry. First rung 1h — see §6.

   pro bono legal advice and donated goods; 4 said "Amount not disclosed". The
   branch existed on 1 of 15 call sites. Now in `formatRange`, plus BriefingView,
   `agent/reason.ts` and the MCP adapter.

   `deadline_cycle`, `is_rolling` may be superseded by a fresher GROUNDED read
   that REMOVES a claim. `set_at` was already on every provenance entry, so no
   migration. Fixed ScottishPower, stale behind admin trust 100 for the second time.

6. **`selfResolving()`** — can a reason stop being true on its own. `false` does
   NOT mean retire: `meta_refresh` is not self-resolving and the fix is in the tag.

## 5. Two things I got wrong, and what they cost

**The index propagation was an inference.** 71 rows took their index from a
sibling on the same domain. Spot-checked five at Paul's request: 3 good, 2 wrong,
both Social Investment Business rows pointing at the bare homepage. 73 of 191
rows carried a bare origin. **Clearing them moved Live and wrong 66 → 75: nine
real findings were being suppressed by a homepage posing as a funding index.**
Cleared, not corrected — an empty index means the guard declines to act.

**I claimed a `js_shell` inference and withdrew it within the hour.** "Large HTML
plus no text" was generalised from two examples and both were something else:
southwark.gov.uk refuses production's egress, wellcome.org read fine an hour
later. `js_shell` is not self-resolving, so it would have stopped a watcher
permanently. **Naming a permanent fault needs positive evidence of that fault.**
