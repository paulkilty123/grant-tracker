Component states — for sign-off

These don't exist in the brand yet, so I've built them. Once you approve, they go into the written spec and every one of the six pages inherits them. Three of the values I used in the earlier mockups turned out to fail contrast — corrected here and flagged below.

Three corrections

I ran the maths on every foreground/background pair rather than eyeballing it. The landing page's own greens are excellent — 10.55:1 for deep on cream. The greys and hairlines were the problem.

Two judgement calls, not bugs.
Ghost button border stays at deep @ 22% (1.49:1) — that's your live landing style, and the rule only bites when the border is the sole thing identifying the control. Here the label is 10.55:1 and carries it. It does mean the focus ring is doing real work, so it can't be dropped.
Terracotta #D67558 is 2.85:1 on cream — fine as a fill, accent or icon, never as text. That's why danger became the deepened #B4472A rather than terra itself.

Buttons — four levels, six states

Focus is a 2px deep outline at 3px offset on every level — one rule, no exceptions, and it must survive keyboard-only navigation. Loading keeps the button's width so the layout doesn't jump.
