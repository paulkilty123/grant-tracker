I wrote "Space Grotesk throughout". That's wrong, and I'd have had the Code session flatten a deliberate system. Your docs/brand-guidelines.md specifies three faces with fixed roles, and the live landing page confirms it still holds: headings, labels, buttons and numerics are Space Grotesk; body prose is Plus Jakarta Sans (17px/26 on the landing page); DM Serif Display is decorative, reserved for the onboarding welcome headline and the About testimonial.

What actually changes is colour, radius, borders and button style. That makes Band A meaningfully smaller than I told you. Corrected spec is attached.

Three things worth changing while we're in here

1. A heading collides. /auth/forgot-password opens with "Reset your password", and so does the confirm phase of /auth/reset-password — same words, different pages, different meaning, and a user hitting a dead link sees the second one right after the first. I've renamed the confirm phase to "You're nearly there" below. Copy change, your call.

2. The expired state shouldn't look like a failure. Per docs/password-reset-flow.md, the everyday cause is Outlook and M365 safe-links prefetching and burning the token before the human clicks — and your users are heavily M365. That's not user error, it's routine. So it gets a neutral gold badge rather than a red one, and the resend field is the primary action rather than an afterthought.

3. The one-per-hour limit needs to stay visible. resetPasswordForEmail is rate limited to roughly one per hour per address, and a second request inside that hour sends nothing silently. Your existing "Not arrived?" box already says this — keep it, it's doing real work.

One state I could not see, and would want to

recovery-link.ts explains that PKCE ties ?code= to the browser that requested the reset, so opening the email on a different device fails with a missing-verifier error — and the page "now explains rather than reporting as a generic expiry". Opening the email on your phone when you requested it on your laptop is an extremely common thing to do, so this state will be seen. I couldn't reach its copy to check whether it needs the same treatment as the expired state. Worth confirming it doesn't render as a bare error.
