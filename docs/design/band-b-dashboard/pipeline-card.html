The proportion bar is the part I'd argue for hardest

Four equal-width tiles give every stage the same visual weight, so £500k and £15k look like comparable quantities. They aren't: £500k is 79% of your entire pipeline and it's sitting in Identified — the stage that means "noticed, not acted on". The bar makes that legible in one glance, and it's the single most useful thing this card could tell a fundraiser.

It's a stacked proportion of one whole, so it takes the ladder's own tones rather than new hues — the same greys and greens, in stage order, with a 2px gap between segments. Won is absent because it's zero; when it lands it appears at the dark end and the bar visibly shifts.

Why not just make the tiles proportional widths? Because a stage at £0 would vanish entirely, and the labels wouldn't fit at 2%. Tiles stay equal so every stage is always readable and clickable; the bar carries the magnitude.

What else changed

Tiles are 132px with the value pinned to the bottom. Label at the top, value at the base, so the four numbers sit on one line regardless of label length. Values go 21px → 30px.

"1 opportunity" three times is noise. In A it stays, because there's room. In B it drops to a bare numeral — and honestly that's the better version of the information either way; the word is doing no work when it's the same on every tile.

Declined comes out of the footer. It's a real outcome, not a footnote, and the old placement had it below the fold of the card on a narrow window.

On day one the total reads £0, not a dash. Zero pounds in play is a true and clear statement; a dash is an absence of information. The stage tiles keep dashes because "no value yet" is genuinely different from "£0 of value".

Still worth flagging: that £500k

One opportunity is 79% of the pipeline, and the proportion bar makes it impossible to miss — which is either exactly right or actively unhelpful depending on whether the figure is real. If it's an aspirational entry against a fund nobody has applied to, this card will now advertise it every time the dashboard loads. Worth checking the record before this ships, because the design's whole value here is that it tells the truth loudly.
