The structure: one ladder, four verbs, mapped to the journey

Find → Apply → Strategise → Scale. Each tier is a deeper verb and a different job, not a bigger version of the same thing — and each is anchored by a real cohort voice. The strongest single feature-demand signal in the whole evidence base (the application builder, named unprompted by both David and Philomina, opposite ends of the spectrum) anchors the entry paid tier and gives the smaller-org segment its clearest, most concrete proposition.

Launch order is not ladder order. The Companion (Strategise) launches first — its engine is the v1 reasoning core, and launching it first sets the price anchor at heartland value. The Apply tier ships shortly after, with the v1.x application builder, positioned clearly as "application help," so £25–30 reads as the apply-well tier, not as Grant Tracker's price.

Apply

Application tier

Fast-follow — ships with v1.x builder~£25–30/mo placeholder · cost-bound

Who it's for

Smaller orgs whose pain is the writing — grassroots and stable-small (~£100k–£500k), a founder or part-time fundraiser doing the applications themselves. The segment that named this feature directly.
PhilominaDavid (the tool he asked for)

Their needs (evidence)

The blank page: "starting that first 10% is the hard part… once you start, half the work is done." Eligibility subtlety caught before weeks are wasted (the 80%-women-criteria miss). A strong application matched to the actual guidance. And a place to track what's in flight and when it's due.

Features that answer

Application builder — framework form: reads the funder's guidance + the org profile, produces a structured scaffold with gap-flagging and "what a strong answer needs," for the user to write into in their own voice. Plus full pipeline (stages, tracking, history) and deadline alerts.
The arithmetic that sells it: one grant won that would otherwise have been missed pays for years. The answer to "why not free ChatGPT?" must be on the tin: the builder knows the funder's actual guidance, your verified eligibility position, and what strong applications need — with citations. Structure, not the AI slop funders now screen out. SSE is already distributing a competitor here, so the sector-channel timing matters.
The cost caveat: the builder is the most inference-heavy feature on the roadmap. The framework design (structure + prompts, not full drafting) is inherently cheaper, and bounded builds-per-month keep the tail contained — but £25–30 is a target to confirm against instrumented cost once the builder exists, not a fixed promise.

The Companion

The launch paid tier — your focus~£65–95/mo placeholder

Who it's for

The paying heartland: £500k–£2m+ charities with a dedicated fundraising lead, and trading social enterprises / co-ops with income targets. The buyer Fundin priced out at £500/mo and free tools underserve.
Jenthe orgs to recruit now

Their needs (evidence)

Capacity relief, not more output (the load-reduction warning); the funder deep-dive time-sink collapsed ("is this worth our time?"); the funding mix de-risked (the £20–25k multi-year gap, concentration risk); deadline vigilance; audit-grade eligibility; strategic counsel on demand.

Features that answer

Everything in Apply, plus the goal agent: goal wizard → reasoned dashboard (pacing, gaps, risk); agent prioritisation with the why; 7-step eligibility audit with citations; funder briefs + decision timelines; selective high-signal digest; the conversation loop. The trial showcases this tier.
The pitch, in their arithmetic: less than half a consultant day per month; hours of funder research collapsed weekly; concentration risk watched continuously. Conversion strengthens once the application builder (shared down to the Apply tier) lands — stage expectations: reasoning-only converts modestly, the full "assistant does the work" lands at v1.x.
Your energy (the majority): the v1 reasoning core IS this tier's engine — build it to the consistency bar against heartland situations. Recruit 2–3 more Jen-type orgs into the cohort now so the autumn trial test is informative. Write all paid-tier language in this buyer's terms.
