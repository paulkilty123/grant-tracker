## Outstanding, in the order I would take them

**1. Eight write-up figures that need prose read, not a rule.** `amount_ungrounded`
rows where the figure genuinely is not on the funder's page: Gannochy £5,000,000,
St Giles £53,737, Community Action Fund £60,000, Suffolk Giving £30,000, Main
Grants £45,000, Energy Resilience £10,000, Rudbaxton £6,000, Riverside £500.
Some are true and sourced elsewhere, some invented. Reading the sentence around
each is the only way to tell.

**2. Two amount corrections held deliberately.** Community Action Fund, where
"£20,000 per year for up to three years" would have become a flat £20,000, and
Brighton & Hove Community Catalyst, where the quote is past tense about a closed
round and the row is the 2027 fund. Both still flagged.

**3. Three rows whose link is a Charity Commission register record.** DCR Allen,
Djanogly, Mackintosh. Those funders publish no website at all, so the register
entry with its correspondent address is the only public route. Keep them or
withdraw them: a product judgement, not a verification one.

**4. Forty-five wrong-fund rows still flagged.** Of the original 83: 12 links
repaired, 21 decided and applied, and what remains is the 16 where the check
looks wrong but the quote was too weak to clear on, and 27 where the fund is on
the site but the page we proposed failed a second read. Neither group misleads
anyone; both are link-quality work.

**5. Nineteen watchlist entries with no baseline.** Seven have a stored URL that
404s, including both Wolfson rows, Art Fund, Dulverton, Heritage Crafts,
Nationwide Foundation and New European Bauhaus. Five sit on hosts the reader
proxy also refuses and look like dead domains. The rest are recent additions
waiting for their first read.

**6. Place-based discovery, drafted and not built.**
`docs/discovery-place-queries.md`. The decision is cadence, not wording: 63
places at one query a day is 63 days, which is too slow for September. A one-off
backfill of the whole list is about £4.50, then one a day to maintain. **If it is
going to produce anything users see by launch it has to run in the first week of
September**, because a fund discovered on the 20th is not visible until it has
been reviewed.

**7. Nothing enrols a funder's INDEX page on the watchlist.** Migration 057's
trigger only ever enrols a row's own `apply_url`, so a brand new fund from a
funder we already carry is invisible until someone adds the row by hand.
`funding_index_url` already holds the index for 100+ live rows, so the data to
enrol from mostly exists. Found by the parallel session; nobody is holding it.
This is the other half of intake and it matters most in September, when rounds
reopen.

**8. Microsoft for Nonprofits still shows £3,500**, pinned by Paul on 1 June.
Their page today offers "$2,000 USD annual service credits". Only he can change a
pinned value, and it needs a decision rather than a correction: whether that was
a different offer or a currency slip.

## The thread running through the whole session

Four separate failures, one shape: **a check that returned something plausible
instead of admitting it had nothing.**

- A page-read verdict thrown away by a guard, so a row stayed live for ten days
  after the engine said its link was wrong.
- A brief written from the first 12,000 characters of a 72,000 character page,
  describing past grantees instead of the offer.
- A watchlist fingerprint taken from a 553-character fragment of an 84,000
  character page, stable and blind for ever.
- A 200 from a site that rewrites unknown paths to a search, read as proof a page
  exists.

Each was only visible because the previous fix stopped the earlier one lying. The
rule that catches all four is in CLAUDE.md already: state the check as a sentence
about the user, then see whether the condition actually tests it.
